1. QUICK START: LOAD THE EXTENSION (30 SECONDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Open your Chromium-based browser (Google Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Opera).

Step 2: Navigate to your extensions page by typing this into your address bar:
        chrome://extensions/
        (In Brave: brave://extensions/ | In Edge: edge://extensions/)

Step 3: In the top-right corner, toggle ON "Developer mode".

Step 4: Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner.

Step 5: In the file dialog, navigate to your cloned Vigil folder and select:
        Frontend/dist
        (e.g., C:\path\to\Vigil\Frontend\dist)

Step 6: "Vigil — Web Trust & Safety Shield" will appear in your extensions list!
        Pin the Vigil shield icon to your browser toolbar for quick access.

* Note: After rebuilding or pulling updates, simply click the Reload (🔄) icon
  on the Vigil card in chrome://extensions/ and refresh your tab.

2. HOW TO USE VIGIL'S DEFENSIVE FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open any website (e.g. https://amazon.com, https://reddit.com, or news sites),
and click the Vigil shield icon in your toolbar.

You will see the popup with 3 primary forensic tabs:

======================================================================
TAB 1: 🛡️ OVERVIEW (Trust Score & Dark Patterns)
======================================================================
- View the unified Trust Score Gauge (0 to 100).
- View the before-and-after Privacy Grade (e.g. D+ -> A-).
- Correlated Findings: Lists detected dark patterns, manufactured countdown
  timers, sneaky subscription checkboxes, or deceptive pricing tricks.
- Strict Privacy: Enable MAIN-world stealth anti-fingerprinting defense
  (Canvas, WebGL, AudioContext, and Hardware persona normalization).
- Master Switch: Pause or resume Vigil protection globally with one click.


======================================================================
TAB 2: 🍪 COOKIES (Live Forensics & Behavioral DNA)
======================================================================
- See the exact number of active cookies stored by the current site.
- Categorized by Behavioral DNA:
  * 🛡️ Safe / Essential: Session tokens, bot protection (Cloudflare).
  * 📊 Analytics: Google Analytics (_ga, _gid), Microsoft Clarity.
  * 🎯 Marketing: Facebook Pixel (_fbp, fr), Google DoubleClick (IDE).
  * ⚙️ Functional: Language preferences, theme state.
- Plain-English Explanations: Click any cookie to view its Shannon entropy,
  transmission security flags (🔒 HTTPS Secure, 🛡️ HttpOnly, SameSite), and
  cryptographic JWT verification.
- Third-Party Embedded Trackers: Scroll down to view external tracking
  scripts and domains currently loaded on the page.


======================================================================
TAB 3: ⚖️ TERMS (Deep Legal & Policy Audit)
======================================================================
- Vigil automatically discovers all legal links in the page footer:
  * Terms of Service / Terms & Conditions
  * Privacy Policy / Privacy Statement
  * Cookie Policy & Disclaimers
- How to Audit:
  1. Select a document from the "Discovered Policies" dropdown.
  2. Click "Deep Audit Selected Policy".
  3. Vigil parses clauses offline and categorizes each into:
     - 🚨 Tricky Terms: Forced binding arbitration, class-action waivers,
       data sale, unilateral price changes, or AI training on your data.
     - ⚠️ Notice Terms: Third-party sharing, retention periods, disclaimers.
     - 🛡️ Fair Terms: Explicit non-sale promises, deletion rights.
  4. Click "Locate on Page" to scroll and highlight the clause in yellow!


======================================================================
EXPLAIN MODE & 1-CLICK RESTORE (NEW IN V2.1)
======================================================================
Vigil operates under a strict "Evidence ≠ Intent" and "Detect ≠ Mutate" rule:
- Why Vigil Acted: Plain-English breakdown showing exact observed facts.
- What Vigil Did NOT Conclude: Transparency into rejected inferences.
- 1-Click Restore: If any visual intervention affects your browsing flow,
  click "Restore Original" to instantly revert DOM modifications.

3. HOW TO RUN THE AUTOMATED TEST SUITE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

From the repository root in your terminal (PowerShell, Bash, or Command Prompt):

A. Run Unit & Integration Suite (Vitest — 51 suites):
   npm test

B. Run Browser Acceptance Suite (Playwright in real Chromium — 13 scenarios):
   npm run test:browser

C. Run Complete Verification Battery:
   npm run test:all

D. Compile Production Extension Bundle:
   npm run build

* For test methodologies and verification reports, see docs/testing/ and
  docs/release/V2.1-RC1-CERTIFICATION.md.

4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Does Vigil send any browsing data to external servers?
A: ABSOLUTELY NOT. Vigil has an unconditional ZERO-TELEMETRY policy. All threat
   detection, dark pattern analysis, legal parsing, and anti-fingerprinting
   execute 100% locally inside your browser process.

Q: Can Vigil see HttpOnly cookies?
A: Yes. With standard browser extension permissions, Vigil inspects both
   JavaScript-accessible cookies and secure HttpOnly cookies locally.

Q: How do I report a security issue or suggest an improvement?
A: See SECURITY.md for coordinated disclosure or CONTRIBUTING.md to submit a PR.
