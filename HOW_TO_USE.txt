  1. INSTALL THE EXTENSION (30 SECONDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: Open Chrome, Brave, or Edge.

  Step 2: Go to chrome://extensions/
          (Brave: brave://extensions/ | Edge: edge://extensions/)

  Step 3: Toggle ON "Developer mode" (top-right corner).

  Step 4: Click "Load unpacked" (top-left).

  Step 5: Select the Frontend/dist folder inside this repository.

  Step 6: Pin the Vigil shield icon to your toolbar.

  That's it. Vigil is now protecting every page you visit.

  * After rebuilding or pulling updates, click the reload icon on
    the Vigil card in chrome://extensions/ and refresh your tab.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2. THE THREE TABS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Click the Vigil shield icon on any website to open the popup.

  TAB 1: OVERVIEW
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Trust Score gauge (0–100) with letter grade
  • Correlated findings: dark patterns, fake urgency, hidden fees
  • Strict Privacy toggle: enables MAIN-world anti-fingerprinting
    (Canvas, WebGL, Audio, Hardware persona normalization)
  • Master switch: pause/resume Vigil globally

  TAB 2: COOKIES
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Active cookie count with behavioral DNA classification:
    - Essential (session tokens, bot protection)
    - Analytics (Google Analytics, Clarity)
    - Marketing (Facebook Pixel, DoubleClick)
    - Functional (language, theme)
  • Click any cookie for Shannon entropy, security flags
    (Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite), and JWT verification
  • Third-party embedded tracker domains at the bottom

  TAB 3: LEGAL
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Vigil discovers legal links automatically (ToS, Privacy
    Policy, Cookie Policy, Disclaimers)
  • Select a document and click "Deep Audit Selected Policy"
  • Clauses are categorized:
    - Tricky: forced arbitration, data sale, AI training rights
    - Notice: third-party sharing, retention periods
    - Fair: non-sale promises, deletion rights
  • Click "Locate on Page" to scroll and highlight in yellow

  NOTE: All analysis runs locally on your device by default.
  External ToS;DR lookups are strictly opt-in and off by default.


  EXPLAIN MODE & 1-CLICK RESTORE
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Why Vigil Acted: plain-English breakdown of observed facts
  • What Vigil Did NOT Conclude: rejected alternative inferences
  • 1-Click Restore: instantly reverts any DOM intervention

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4. FAQ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Q: Does Vigil send browsing data to external servers?
  A: No. Vigil has an unconditional zero-telemetry policy. All detection,
     analysis, and defense runs locally inside your browser.

  Q: What about the ToS;DR legal lookup?
  A: It is strictly opt-in, off by default, and gated by a non-bypassable
     consent check at the network call site (not just a UI toggle).
     When enabled, only the public domain name is sent. No cookies,
     credentials, or user identifiers are transmitted.

  Q: Can Vigil see HttpOnly cookies?
  A: Yes, via standard chrome.cookies API permissions (read-only, local).

  Q: How do I report a security issue?
  A: See SECURITY.md for coordinated disclosure guidelines.
