1. QUICK START: LOAD THE EXTENSION (30 SECONDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Open your Chromium-based browser (Google Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Opera).

Step 2: Type this in your address bar and press Enter:
        chrome://extensions/
        (In Brave: brave://extensions/ | In Edge: edge://extensions/)

Step 3: In the top-right corner, turn ON "Developer mode".

Step 4: Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner.

Step 5: In the folder selector dialog, select the following folder:
        D:\Browex\Frontend\dist

Step 6: You will see "Vigil — Web Trust & Safety Shield" appear in your list!
        Pin the Vigil icon (shield) to your browser toolbar for easy access.

* Note: Whenever you make changes or rebuild, simply click the reload icon (🔄)
  on the Vigil card in chrome://extensions/.

2. HOW TO USE VIGIL'S FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open any website (e.g. https://www.india.gov.in/, https://amazon.com, or
https://reddit.com), and click the Vigil shield icon in your toolbar.

You will see a clean popup with 3 primary tabs:

======================================================================
TAB 1: 🛡️ OVERVIEW (Trust Score & Dark Patterns)
======================================================================
- View the unified Trust Score Gauge (0 to 100).
- See the before-and-after Privacy Grade (e.g. C -> A).
- Correlated Findings: Lists detected dark patterns, fake countdown timers,
  sneaky subscription checkboxes, or deceptive pricing tricks on the page.
- Strict Privacy Button: Click to enable the MAIN-world stealth anti-
  fingerprinting defense on this site.


======================================================================
TAB 2: 🍪 COOKIES (Live Forensics & Tracker Inventory)
======================================================================
- See the exact number of active cookies stored by the site.
- Filter by category:
  * 🛡️ Safe / Essential: Session tokens, bot protection (Cloudflare).
  * 📊 Analytics: Google Analytics (_ga, _gid), Microsoft Clarity.
  * 🎯 Marketing: Facebook Pixel (_fbp, fr), Google DoubleClick (IDE).
  * ⚙️ Functional: Language preferences, theme choices.
- Plain-English Explanations: Click any cookie to see what it does, why it
  was set, its security flags (🔒 HTTPS Secure, 🛡️ HttpOnly), and its lifespan.
- Third-Party Embedded Trackers: Scroll down to view external tracking
  scripts and domains currently loaded on the page.
- Click the 🔄 icon anytime to refresh live cookies.


======================================================================
TAB 3: ⚖️ TERMS (Deep Legal & Terms Audit)
======================================================================
- Vigil automatically discovers all legal links in the page footer:
  * Terms of Service / Terms & Conditions
  * Privacy Policy / Privacy Statement
  * Cookie Policy / Website Policies
  * User Agreements & Disclaimers
- How to Audit:
  1. Select a document from the "Discovered Policies" dropdown.
  2. Click "Deep Audit Selected Policy".
  3. Vigil fetches the text, breaks it down into clauses, and categorizes
     each clause into:
     - 🚨 Tricky Terms: Forced binding arbitration, class-action waivers,
       data sale, or using your content to train AI models.
     - ⚠️ Notice Terms: Third-party sharing, retention periods, disclaimers.
     - 🛡️ Fair Terms: Explicit non-sale promises, deletion rights, etc.
  4. Click "Locate on Page" to highlight any clause directly in the document!

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: I updated the code, but my browser still shows the old extension behavior?
A: In chrome://extensions/, find Vigil and click the round Reload icon (🔄).
   Then refresh the webpage you are testing.

Q: Can Vigil see cookies that are HttpOnly?
A: Yes! Vigil has the "cookies" extension permission, allowing it to inspect
   both JavaScript-accessible cookies and secure HttpOnly cookies.

Q: Does Vigil send my browsing history to any server?
A: NO. Vigil has a strict ZERO-TELEMETRY policy. All heuristic threat analysis,
   legal clause classification, and fingerprint perturbation happen 100%
   inside your browser on your device.

Q: How do I report an issue or suggest a feature?
A: Open an issue on GitHub at https://github.com/ath9rv/Vigil or email
   mailxatharv@gmail.com.
